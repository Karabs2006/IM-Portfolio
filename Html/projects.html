Game Development

Infected Wilds

This is Infected Wilds, my very first fully built video game. It is a top-down 2D adventure shooter where players take on the role of a park ranger investigating strange occurrences within a mysterious forest. Developed as a group project over the course of several months, the game marked my first experience working on a large-scale game development project from start to finish. I gained valuable experience in teamwork and development workflows. The game’s narrative and thematic direction were heavily inspired by the Resident Evil franchise, particularly its focus on suspense and use of environmental storytelling.
